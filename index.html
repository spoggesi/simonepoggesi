About Me

My path into food science began with a curiosity for applied chemistry at technical high school and evolved through viticulture, oenology, and biosensor research in France. A career-defining pivot occurred during my Ph.D. when I took over our laboratory's sensory panels. That transition led me to Massey University's Feast Lab in New Zealand alongside Prof. Joanne Hort, where I led industry consumer research and served as Principal Investigator on competitive grants integrating virtual environments (VR), AI/NLP thematic analysis, and implicit consumer testing.

Immersing myself in New Zealand reshaped both my personal relationship with food—cooking from scratch—and my leadership philosophy: bridging diverse cultures while guiding team members so everyone feels genuinely seen, valued, and empowered. Returning to industry as R&D Manager at Giotto Consulting in Italy provided a pragmatic reality check on research market value. Crossing countries, disciplines, and sectors has made me an adaptable researcher who bridges technical food engineering, qualitative consumer insights, and empathetic team leadership.
