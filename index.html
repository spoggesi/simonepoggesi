About Me

My path into food science began with a curiosity for applied chemistry at technical high school and evolved through viticulture, oenology, and biosensor research in France. A career-defining pivot occurred during my Ph.D. when I took over our laboratory's sensory panels. That transition led me to Massey University's Feast Lab in New Zealand alongside Prof. Joanne Hort, where I led industry consumer research and served as Principal Investigator on competitive grants (> $30,000 NZD) integrating virtual environments (VR), AI/NLP thematic analysis, and implicit consumer testing[cite: 18, 19, 21, 24].

Immersing myself in New Zealand reshaped both my personal relationship with food—cooking from scratch—and my leadership philosophy: bridging diverse cultures while guiding team members so everyone feels truly seen, valued, and empowered. Returning to industry as R&D Manager at Giotto Consulting in Italy provided a pragmatic reality check on research market value. Crossing countries, disciplines, and sectors has made me an adaptable researcher who bridges technical food engineering, qualitative consumer insights, and empathetic team leadership.

Grants & Research Leadership

Principal Investigator | MURF Grant ($24,800 NZD)

Rapid Lexicon Development for Functional Foods

Pioneered an agile, high-ecological-validity Home-Use Testing (HUT) methodology for functional beverages. Integrated AI-assisted thematic analysis (NVivo), Natural Language Processing (NLP), implicit Response Time Tasks (RTT), and consumer cognitive segmentation (analytic vs. holistic) with industry engagement (Fonterra, FoodHQ)[cite: 21, 22, 23, 24].

Principal Investigator | REaDI Fund ($5,000 NZD)

The Food Metaverse: Exploring Consumer Insights

Executed cross-cultural qualitative studies on virtual food experiences and digital dining across Indigenous Māori and European cohorts using Meta Quest 3 VR, Whisper-X automated transcription, and thematic coding[cite: 18, 19, 20].
